# Before an agent says the task is done

A-W1-public-engineering-r2 is a newly authored public variant. The controlled A-W1 workshop and reserved assessments remain unchanged. No learner validation is claimed.

## Write five lines

- Action completed: what did the tool actually return?
- Result required: whose need or constraint must be met?
- Evidence: what resulting state was checked independently of the tool transcript?
- Remaining limitation: what is unresolved, and who may accept it?
- Stopping rule: which bounded next check is authorized, and when must the agent hand off?

## Worked case / newly authored fiction

- Tool response: reservation R-17 succeeded. Venue fixture: reservation exists. Two members report they cannot attend because their last bus leaves before the event ends. Others have planned around the original time.
- Grant: check one alternative time with the venue; do not change the booking. Completion would overstate the result; rescheduling would exceed authority.
- New fictional venue reply: no alternative time exists for this room that day. Moving within that room and day is removed. Travel, other venues and other dates have not been exhausted.
- Handoff: room reserved and unchanged; two members still cannot attend; the authorized check is complete. Coordinator: authorize a travel check, consider another venue, or keep the booking with its limitation explicit.

## Compare alternatives on the same dimensions

- KEEP — People: existing plans preserved, two excluded. Unknown: acceptable travel option. Authority: coordinator decides whether to retain with limitation explicit.
- MOVE — People: might help two, might disrupt others. Unknown: acceptable different venue/date. Authority: coordinator must authorize any booking change.
- After the new fact, moving within this room and day is unavailable. That does not resolve the attendance conflict or justify changing some other booking.

## Evaluate the report separately from the outing

- Supported facts: reservation exists; no unsupported claim that everyone can attend.
- Unmet requirement visible: attendance conflict explicitly reported.
- Authority respected: no booking change.
- Inquiry bounded: one authorized check completed, then stopped and handed off.
- A corrected handoff can pass these behavioral checks while the required outing result remains unresolved. This is an illustrative rubric over authored fields, not an agent benchmark.

## Fresh public exercise

- An agent creates a support ticket and reports the customer's issue fixed. The ticket exists, but the customer has not tested the proposed repair. The agent may request one diagnostic result; it may not close the ticket. Write its corrected report and stopping rule.
- Separate ticket creation, evidence of a working repair, the permitted inquiry and the decision to close. Do not invent a customer response.
