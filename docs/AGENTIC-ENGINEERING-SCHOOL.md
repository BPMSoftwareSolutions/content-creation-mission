# Agentic Engineering: From Autonomous Agents to Governed Capabilities

The signature class turns the capability series into a learning progression.
Its teaching frame is intent → scenario → authority → agent responsibility →
capability and provider → execution → testimony → evaluation → governed outcome.
It is a SideFX curriculum, not a claim that this is an established industry standard.

The first worked module uses Episode 1, its existing 1080p film, the reviewed
material circuit and the exact target-interlock rules. Nine subsequent films
remain planned; their learning objectives, capability sources, exercises and
defense artifacts are mapped now. Six broader school pathways remain a roadmap.

## Learning experience

1. **Frame:** inspect nine engineering questions, each with an Episode 1 example
   and a concrete failure to challenge.
2. **Watch:** follow the human story and jump to the current adjudication and
   target proof chapters inside the completed film.
3. **Inspect:** play the source-bound certification circuit. The silver ball
   preserves split, delayed arrival and ALL-join semantics. Required testimony
   remains a gap after the intended outcome appears.
4. **Predict:** choose a request and evidence assumption, predict the authority
   decision, then compare it with the exact authored target rules.
5. **Substitute:** change the illustrative provider. Eligible providers preserve
   the decision and responsibility. An incompatible or unavailable provider
   prevents even a permitted request from reaching the simulated effect stage.
6. **Defend:** author a boundary brief, identify missing evidence, self-review it
   and export the draft with its exercise comparisons for instructor defense.

## Episode contract extension

Every future worked lesson should supply these educational projections alongside
its content package:

| Projection | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Engineering concept | The precise distinction the capability teaches. |
| Human scenario | Input, Event, Outcome and the experience at stake. |
| Current evidence | What exact source artifacts establish, with explicit limits. |
| Intended design | The experience the capability should eventually realize. |
| Interactive challenge | A consequential prediction, a changed condition and explanatory feedback. |
| Learner artifact | A reusable engineering document, map, contract or evidence inspection. |
| Defense rubric | Questions that expose unsupported claims and authority expansion. |
| Platform gap | A bounded future mechanic or proof obligation, separated from current behavior. |

The instructor guide gives each of the ten studies its artifact and defense
question. Assessment uses four dimensions: meaning, authority separation,
provider substitution and scoped proof. Each dimension can be scored 0 (absent),
1 (asserted) or 2 (specific and supported). A missing dimension requires revision.
Self-checks are not instructor scores or professional credentials.

## Source and execution boundaries

`declarations/agentic-engineering-course.json` is editorial authority for the
learning surface. It pins the supplied direction memo, season, capability page,
ten capability content contracts, target design and gap report. The compiler
validates those bytes and reuses the existing capability composition gate.

The market memo is research context. Its pricing, credential and monitoring
statements do not become product facts or operating instructions by inclusion.
See [the focused source check](agentic-engineering-market-check.md) for the claims
used to inform positioning. The class describes its own teaching focus without
claiming category exclusivity or institutional endorsement.

The lab's provider compatibility and availability are explicit instructional
assumptions. They extend the teaching exercise, not the current capsule's
execution behavior. The decision uses the existing target rules verbatim.
Current-evidence mode forces live-boundary proof to false. Target mode cannot
erase an explicitly missing proof, uncovered path or identity drift in a case.
Only PERMIT plus a compatible, available illustrative provider can display an
illustrative inspection result. No branch can publish or expand authority.

Exports are `LEARNER_DESIGN_TESTIMONY`, retain exact source hashes and state
`SELF_REVIEW_ONLY_INSTRUCTOR_DEFENSE_REQUIRED`. Open fields stay visible. A saved
draft is local to the browser and exact course edition. Restored comparisons are
recomputed against the pinned rules; imported decisions are not trusted.
