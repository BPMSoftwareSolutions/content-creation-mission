# Audience evidence belongs in direction

The unit of direction is the complete human experience. Persistent people,
artifacts and service entities change state and relationship across Input,
Event and Outcome. Capsule mechanics constrain what happens; they are not a
replacement for the human subject.

Every directed film carries an audience profile: demand theme, audience,
human stake, hook variants, curiosity question, visual triggers, intensity
curve, emotional arc, payoff and share hypothesis. These begin as creative
hypotheses. Demand, retention, sharing and conversion remain unknown until
observed. Never fabricate an uplift, benchmark, production outage or recovery
time to strengthen a hook.

Concentrate intensity where action warrants it. Allow the outcome to settle.
The first experiment uses 6, 7, 9, 8, 6, 5 across six shots. Those numbers are
direction, not predictive scores. A hypothesis may be revised without changing
source meaning or inventing capability behavior.

YouTube's official guidance distinguishes appeal, engagement and satisfaction,
and recommends an opening that fulfills the title/thumbnail promise:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650?hl=en (verified 2026-09-05).
This informs the experiment; it does not establish demand for the topic.
The supplied ScienceDirect paper was inaccessible and is not used as evidence.

Measure platform, variant, publication and observation window alongside
impressions, views, CTR, retention, shares, subscriber conversion and clicks.
Fractional metrics use 0–1. Missing values are unknown, never zero. Compare
only comparable cohorts; descriptive differences are not causal conclusions.
Keep the observation CSV empty before publication. Local reviewer interaction
is not audience evidence. Publishing or sending to an audience is a separate
action and has not happened in this lab.

First deliverable: samples/narration-continuity/index.html. The film is an
editorial local-demo composition: A failure is simulated, B fails a declared
audio compatibility check, C synthesizes actual speech, and one local job
completes. It does not claim managed end-to-end integration or zero downtime.
