# Visual taxonomy

Version 1: explicit lexical rules; editorial hypotheses, not statistical clusters. Ties remain ambiguous. Review scores are not probabilities.

## admission

A candidate is evaluated at an authority boundary.

Motif: candidate and labeled boundary

Examples: adapt-job-market-intelligence-evidence::verify-jmi-type-admission; admit-canonical-circuit-blueprint::admit-canonical-circuit-blueprint; admit-capability-authority::evaluate-capability-authority-admission

## resolution

A declared question or candidate set resolves to a supported result.

Motif: candidate cards around one result

Examples: admit-canonical-circuit-blueprint::emit-immutable-blueprint-authority; admit-capsule-execution-closure::verify-declared-dependency-closure; analyze-sidefx-semantic-impact::analyze-sidefx-semantic-impact

## routing

Declared paths determine a permitted continuation.

Motif: labeled route map

Examples: author-capability-scenario-conveyor::hold-capability-scenario-selection-with-no-eligible-unit; author-capability-scenario-conveyor::retain-conveyed-route-state; author-capability-scenario-conveyor::resolve-authorized-scenario-continuation

## transformation

Input representation changes into an output representation.

Motif: before and after forms with preserved identity

Examples: project-model-provider-protocol::normalize-provider-http-failure-testimony; project-model-provider-protocol::normalize-provider-transport-testimony; project-model-provider-protocol::normalize-model-response-format

## projection

One source meaning is embodied in a derived representation.

Motif: authority document and derived view

Examples: author-canonical-circuit-blueprint-candidate::bind-blueprint-design-testimony; construct-consumer-projection-plan::construct-consumer-projection-plan; construct-model-connection-runtime-closure::reject-runtime-digest-drift

## recovery

A degraded condition has a declared recovery response.

Motif: interrupted structure and recovery candidate

Examples: execute-governed-model-invocation::prevent-undeclared-model-attempt-or-substitution; transact-governed-tooling-responsibility-binding::complete-governed-tooling-binding-transaction

## comparison

Two representations are compared against a criterion.

Motif: aligned evidence panels

Examples: compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle::compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle; compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle::resolve-projected-tooling-migration-oracle-comparison-scope; compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle::compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle-observations

## observation

A state or evidence relationship becomes available to inspection.

Motif: revealed evidence layers

Examples: author-capability-scenario-conveyor::inspect-one-scenario-authority-fragment; compare-projected-tooling-migration-oracle::observe-frozen-tooling-migration-operational-oracle; construct-capability-author-delegate-request::observe-delegate-canonical-feature

## publication

An artifact gains a declared publication disposition.

Motif: artifact and publication boundary

Examples: construct-capability-author-delegate-request::publish-capability-author-delegate-request; construct-projectable-capability-publication-shape::construct-projectable-capability-publication-shape; deliver-capability-change-api::transition-capability-change-through-api

## validation

Evidence is checked against declared requirements.

Motif: evidence beside requirement slots

Examples: adapt-job-market-intelligence-evidence::verify-jmi-record-binding; admit-canonical-circuit-blueprint::require-blueprint-conformance-evidence; admit-capsule-execution-closure::verify-closure-digest

## convergence

A join requires a declared collection of inputs.

Motif: required product slots at a join

Examples: govern-authoring-convergence::govern-authoring-convergence; govern-authoring-convergence::resolve-convergence-disposition; prove-canonical-blueprint-geometry::prove-branch-and-fan-out-distinction

## rejection

A candidate is refused with stated evidence.

Motif: stopped candidate and reason card

Examples: admit-registry-asset::reject-colliding-asset-identity; author-capability-scenario-conveyor::reject-invalid-capability-scenario-authoring-source; author-capability-scenario-conveyor::reject-scenario-authoring-context-scope-expansion

## extraction

Structured information is obtained from a source representation.

Motif: source layers and extracted structure

Examples: extract-semantic-carrier-graph::extract-canonical-carrier-graph; extract-semantic-carrier-graph::return-extraction-held; inspect-delegated-capability-candidate::inspect-declarative-candidate-artifact-slots

## execution

A declared operation is performed.

Motif: operation frame and outcome evidence

Examples: admit-capsule-execution-closure::admit-capsule-execution-closure; admit-capsule-execution-closure::verify-execution-entries; admit-capsule-execution-closure::bind-closure-receipt

## human-approval

A human decision is part of the declared responsibility.

Motif: proposal at a labeled decision station

Examples: admit-registry-asset::require-human-admission-disposition; bind-canonical-blueprint-review::bind-canonical-blueprint-review; bind-canonical-blueprint-review::admit-human-blueprint-review-testimony

## composition

Several declared products contribute to a combined product or required set.

Motif: labeled parts and a shared frame

Examples: assemble-sidefx-capability-authoring-context::assemble-sidefx-capability-authoring-context; author-capability-scenario-conveyor::assemble-closed-capability-source-bundle-from-scenario-fragments; author-tooling-capability-candidate::assemble-canonical-authority-artifacts

## retrieval

Requested information is located in a bounded source.

Motif: bounded collection and found evidence

Examples: resolve-sidefx-semantic-knowledge-request::resolve-sidefx-semantic-knowledge-request
