# Transfer master — facilitator only

**WP-01 v1.** Issue only an assigned, unseen card; strip metadata. Do not show diagnostic or worked-example answers alongside it. All situations are fictional. Record exposure and help under the [procedure](../facilitator-guide.md).

## A-T1 v1 — learner card

Library returns were logged, but a requested book cannot be found for the next reader. What has happened, what result does the next reader need, and what would you check or do next? Explain the limits of what you can conclude.

## B-T1 v1 — learner card

An undated excerpt is offered as proof that a person still endorses a statement. Distinguish what the excerpt shows from what the claimant infers. What remains unknown, and what information would matter to your conclusion?

## C-T1 v1 — initial learner card

One editor promises to give every request exclusive attention immediately. What do you think of this promise? Explain your initial position using the promise as stated.

## C-T1 v1 — critic card, reveal after saving initial answer

A critic asks how this promise handles two simultaneous requests. Another concludes that no scheduling rule can ever work. Evaluate each argument. Explain what you would retain or change in your initial position and why. Keep the original premises fixed.
