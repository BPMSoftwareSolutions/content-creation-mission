# Diagnostic master — facilitator only

**WP-01 v1.** Each card below is separate. Strip headings/metadata before delivery. All situations are fictional. Collect every diagnostic before teaching. See [delivery and exposure rules](../facilitator-guide.md).

## A-D1 v1 — learner card

Workshop invitations were sent, but some invitees cannot open the instructions. What has happened, what remains unmet, and what should be checked? Explain who is affected by the difference.

## B-D1 v1 — learner card

A dated photograph shows an empty meeting room. What does it support about the room now? State the limits and what new information is needed. Explain your reasoning.

## C-D1 v1 — initial learner card

A club proposes that every decision about an activity, including approving a decision, must first receive a separate approval. Could the club use this rule to start an activity? Explain your initial judgment and any assumptions you need.

## C-D1 v1 — critic card, reveal after saving initial answer

A critic asks: “The first approval is itself a decision. What approves it?” Keep the rule as stated. Explain whether it can get started and what, if anything, should change. You may keep or revise your initial judgment, with reasons.
