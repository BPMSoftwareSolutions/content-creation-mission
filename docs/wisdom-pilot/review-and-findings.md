# Editorial review and pilot findings

**WP-01 v1 · Author self-check; independent review and learner evidence pending.** This document is not a release approval or an empirical report.

## Author's adversary pass

| Review question | Current answer and limit |
|---|---|
| Exact claim? | The three lessons propose to teach completion/result distinctions, bounded interpretation of information, and reasoned reconsideration under fixed-premise challenge. |
| What would falsify it? | Unfamiliar answers fail those distinctions, depend on hints, or confuse correction with capitulation. Current evidence cannot establish effectiveness. |
| Related established work? | The strategy uses worked examples, formative feedback, transfer tasks, and independent reviewer calibration. No external literature review or empirical efficacy claim is supplied by this package; research grounding remains open. |
| What differs? | The local combination and connection to SideFX vocabulary. No novelty or superiority is established. |
| Simplest competing solution? | Plain-language cases and reflection with a facilitator, without branding or a software evaluator. No comparison group is part of this pilot. |
| Failure modes? | Case exposure, leading help, ambiguous words, small samples, overlapping dimensions, uncalibrated substitutes, and fluent repetition mistaken for transfer. |
| Attack paths? | Memorize the strategy's answers; skim the case bank; exploit prompts that signal expected disagreement; agree with the instructor to appear competent. Delivery controls and exposure records reduce but cannot eliminate these issues. |
| Evidence that would support success? | Actual unfamiliar responses with case-specific reasons, preserved dissent and limits, plus independent judgments that can be explained against facts. |
| Evidence present? | Authored scripts, text plates, prompts, exemplars, and empty templates only. No learner data or independent human judgments. |
| What remains unproven? | Transfer, retention, causal effectiveness, reviewer consistency, skill separation, equivalent difficulty, philosophical sufficiency, and applicability beyond this cohort. |

Until those fields have evidence, status remains **validation pending**. A small before/after sample without a comparison cannot establish that instruction caused improvement. Discussion agreement is not independent validation. No outcome permits a certification, learner ranking, numerical pass threshold, automatic academy launch, or claim that all seven proposed lenses are sufficient.
