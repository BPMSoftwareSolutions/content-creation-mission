## Shared editorial decisions

Audience is a proposed single cohort of engineers reviewing consequential software actions; participant selection and actual session dates are pending. Each learner may give a narrative account, use a limited descriptive table, or explain why a model would distort the situation. No circuit is needed for these cases. A chart cannot confer decision rights. Authority is case-specific and sometimes unknown; justified refusal to decide is acceptable.

Human stakes include exclusion from an outing, misrepresentation of group preferences, and unequal burdens in a shared role. Worked examples specify who can change arrangements, who is affected, competing interests, and how to hear dissent. Assessment cards deliberately elicit fewer dimensions; do not penalize a learner for questions they were not asked.

The three [teaching scripts](teaching/lesson-a.md) and corresponding text plates contain prediction, explanation, challenge, alternative, a condition defeating the tempting claim, and a plain-language text equivalent. Read the printed scene and preserve pauses for accessible delivery; the text is also the narration/caption source. Use no mandatory motion, color-only meaning, or timing-dependent reveal. A future film must preserve these states and labels.

## Falsifiers and revision dependencies

The learning claims are undermined if unfamiliar responses repeat the unsupported leaps, rely on hints or vocabulary, or cannot distinguish a valid challenge from a broad unsupported objection. Successful worked-example repetition alone does not support them. A simpler competing intervention is the same ordinary-language prompts with neutral reflection time and no branded teaching; this pilot does not compare interventions and cannot establish causal superiority.

Changing a fact or anchor affects its prompt card, script/plate if used there, feedback, exposure qualification, and review packet. Record the affected IDs and versions in the private session log. Freeze original responses and first reviewer judgments; corrections supplement them rather than overwrite them.

Production owners, independent reviewers, review dispositions, dissent, and actual pilot findings are recorded in [the templates](assessment/record-templates.md) and [review record](review-and-findings.md). All assignments and human decisions are pending.
