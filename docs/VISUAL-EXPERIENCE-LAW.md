# Visual Experience Law

Input establishes the world before. Event depicts the world changing. Outcome
shows the materially different experience afterward. A phase cannot be a
cosmetic restyling of another phase.

Reject as VISUAL_PHASE_COLLAPSE when captions, color, arrows, a scan beam, a
checkmark or a badge supply the only meaningful change. Identity may remain
continuous; camera, blocking, action and experiential state must change.
Review the images without phase labels. A viewer must see cause, behavior and
consequence without reading a caption.

The director fixes each shot before generation: who, where, known information,
unresolved tension, acting responsibility, physical visual behavior, focus,
causality, changed knowledge or condition, beneficiary and visual evidence.
The image provider realizes these decisions; it does not invent the scenario.

For informational capabilities, changed reality may be knowledge, permission or
an explicit reason to wait. Do not fabricate execution, recovery or customer
effects merely to create drama. Human staging is an editorial analogy, explicitly
separate from canonical actors and effects. Working copies may be arranged while
source records remain untouched. A reviewer seeing a decision is not a claim
that the underlying system has performed the authorized work.

Eight review dimensions: phase distinction, human legibility, causal clarity,
outcome closure, identity continuity, semantic fidelity, label independence and
believable operational experience. Each requires an observation; model confidence
or structural checks alone cannot establish visual conformance. Any failure
returns VISUAL_EXPERIENCE_DOES_NOT_CONFORM. Review receipts bind the exact image
hashes. Regeneration invalidates the old receipt.

The original ceramic-tabletop pilot fails this law. Its media remain historical
artifacts and must not be described as the accepted visual system.
